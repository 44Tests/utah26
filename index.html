About the Pre-ECNL: The Pre-ECNL is ECNL's developmental program for U10–U12 players, serving 300+ clubs and 800+ teams across the country. The 28-team field combines cup flight champions from all four qualifying events plus at-large invitations for other top-performing teams. Games are played 9v9. Teams advancing to a championship game play two games on Sunday. Held at Epic Sports Park, 1200 Epic Lane, Provo, UT 84601.

Regional Cup Qualifiers

U11G flight champions from each event earn automatic Champions Cup berths

🏆
Midwest Cup
Sep 27–28, 2025 · Lebanon, OH · Warren County Sports Park
First qualifying event of the season. 72 girls teams competed across U10–U12 age groups in 10 flights. U11G flight winners in each division earned Champions Cup berths. Programs like SLSG, Chicago Inter, Columbus Force, GTFC Impact, and Minnesota Thunder Academy were among the Midwest clubs competing.
🏆
Carolina Cup
Fall 2025 · Southeast US
Second qualifying event of the season. Girls teams from the Southeast and Mid-Atlantic regions competed across U10–U12 age groups. Flight champions in U11G advanced to the Champions Cup. East Coast programs including PDA White, Match Fit Surf, Philadelphia Ukrainian Nationals, and Racing Louisville Academy were positioned in this competitive window.
🏆
East Coast Cup
Winter 2025–26 · East Coast
Third qualifying cup of the season. ECNL confirmed both Boys and Girls champions from the East Coast Cup earned spots in the Champions Cup. Strong East Coast programs from NJ, PA, OH, KY, and NY competed for flight championships across all age groups.
🏆
Northern Cal Cup
Mar 13–15, 2026 · Davis, CA · Davis Legacy Soccer Complex
Final qualifying cup. 54 girls teams competed across U11–U12. Teams played group play Saturday + Sunday with championship games Sunday afternoon. Idaho Rush (Girls Blue) made history as Idaho's first-ever Pre-ECNL Cup Champions. Pleasanton Rage and San Juan SC were among the NorCal programs qualifying.
⭐ Idaho Rush — Idaho's First Pre-ECNL Cup Champions
